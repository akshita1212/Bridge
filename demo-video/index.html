⟡ Bridge

Bridge product demo, and how it is made.

A 1 min 52 s film of the real Bridge app, frozen on its demo project and animated frame by frame. Everything that made it is in this folder: the screens, the film script, the tools, the sound and the outputs. Change the words, the timing or the screens, and render a new cut.

Music credit to add wherever the film is shared: "Music: Sascha Ende, ende.app (CC BY 4.0)".

The scenes

Times are seconds into the film. "Screen" opens the frozen page of the real app that the scene animates; "Player" opens the live film at that moment.

What's in this folder

The film is a web page (film/index.html) whose every frame is a function of time. The tools render it to video.
