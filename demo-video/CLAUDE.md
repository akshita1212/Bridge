# Bridge demo video

This folder holds the Bridge product demo film and everything that made it. Before changing anything here, read
`SKILL.md` (the full map: scenes, where every word, time and screen lives, and the commands), then `STORYBOARD.md`
(the scenes, decisions and the feedback each change came from).

- Most edits are in `film/film.js`: `TL` (timing), `CHAPTERS` (captions), `schedule()` (camera, cursor, notes).
- Show stills before rendering; a full render takes about 30 minutes.
- Keep to the film's rules in `SKILL.md`: real UI only, the space is called Overview, roles are jobs,
  notes line up with their arrows, no footer text on the end card.
